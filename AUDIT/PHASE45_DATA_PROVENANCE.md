## 2. Definitive Provenance Finding

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Audit Finding:** All runtime trade decisions generated by `CanonicalDecisionEngine` derive exclusively from **`REAL`** normalized live market feeds. Offline fallbacks in daily journal forecasting are strictly tagged as `FALLBACK_SYNTHETIC` and excluded from forward statistical edge metrics.
